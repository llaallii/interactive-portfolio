## Project Overview

The Elexy Power Unit project involved comprehensive validation of a safety-critical electromechanical autoinjector for medical applications. As the validation lead, I was responsible for ensuring complete requirements-to-test traceability and validating electrical performance, timing behavior, and automated injection logic according to medical device standards.

## Challenge

Medical device validation presents unique challenges:

- **Regulatory Compliance**: Must meet stringent IEC 62304 and ISO 13485 standards
- **Safety-Critical System**: Any failure could endanger patient safety

- **Time Constraints**: Tight timeline to support production ramp schedule

The autoinjector required precise electrical timing, reliable motor control, and fail-safe mechanisms—all validated to medical device standards.

## Technical Approach

### Requirements Analysis & Traceability

I established a comprehensive traceability system:

```python
# Example: Automated Requirements Traceability Check
import pandas as pd

def validate_rtm_coverage(requirements_df, test_cases_df):
    """Ensure every requirement has at least one test case"""
    
    req_ids = set(requirements_df['req_id'])
    tested_reqs = set(test_cases_df['req_id'])
    
    untested = req_ids - tested_reqs
    coverage = (len(tested_reqs) / len(req_ids)) * 100
    
    return {
        'coverage_percent': coverage,
        'untested_requirements': list(untested),
        'total_requirements': len(req_ids),
        'tested_requirements': len(tested_reqs)
    }

# Automated daily coverage reports
rtm_status = validate_rtm_coverage(requirements, test_cases)
print(f"Current RTM Coverage: {rtm_status['coverage_percent']:.1f}%")
```

## Technical Challenges Overcome

### Challenge 1: Timing Variability

**Problem**: Injection timing varied beyond acceptable limits  
**Solution**: Implemented DOE (Design of Experiments) to identify root cause—battery voltage variation  
**Result**: Added voltage compensation algorithm, reduced timing variance by 75%

### Challenge 2: Environmental Stress

**Problem**: Performance degradation at temperature extremes  
**Solution**: Designed comprehensive environmental test matrix, identified thermal compensation needs  
**Result**: Successfully validated performance across full operating range (-20°C to +50°C)
